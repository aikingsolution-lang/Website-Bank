QUALITY AT EACH PHASE

Evidence over assumptions.

How are findings closed?+

Each finding is assigned an owner, action and target date. Closure is supported by reviewed evidence; unresolved items remain visible in the readiness report.

What can change the timeline?+

The 12–20 week estimate depends on size, branch coverage, stakeholder availability and approval turnaround. Phases can overlap; the project plan is confirmed during scoping.

What are the limits of manual discovery?+

Workshops, questionnaires and sampling are not an exhaustive automated scan. Undisclosed systems or data stores can remain outside discovery. We document assumptions and coverage.

How does the bank take ownership?+

The handover includes editable registers, approved procedures, training records, open actions and an agreed review cadence. Your teams remain responsible for ongoing operation.
