Your DPDP implementation roadmap

A clear path to DPDP readiness.

Seven coordinated phases help your bank turn DPDP requirements into documented controls, trained teams and evidence of readiness.

Indicative engagement timeline

12–20 weeks
Indicative total · phases may overlap

01

Readiness and applicability

2–3 weeks

Agree on your bank’s scope, stakeholders, personal data processing and applicability questions.

Output: Readiness report
02

Gap analysis and risk assessment

2–3 weeks

Identify gaps in current practices and prioritize the actions your bank needs to take.

Output: Gap report & risk register
03

Data inventory and flow mapping

3–4 weeks

Map how personal data moves across departments, systems and third parties.

Output: Inventory & RoPA
04

Policies, notices and procedures

3–4 weeks

Create bank-specific policies, notices and procedures from the assessment findings.

Output: Policy & procedure set
05

Safeguards, vendors and breach readiness

3–5 weeks

Review safeguards and vendors, then rehearse your bank’s breach response.

Output: Review report & vendor register
06

Training and awareness

1–2 weeks

Help leadership and operational teams understand their data protection responsibilities.

Output: Training materials & records
07

Validation and handover

2 weeks

Review sampled evidence, record open actions and present readiness to leadership.

Output: Readiness report & evidence pack

How do you close a finding?+

Each finding has an owner, action and target date. We review the supporting evidence before closure. Unresolved findings remain visible in the readiness report.

What affects the engagement timeline?+

The 12–20 week estimate depends on your bank’s size, branch coverage, stakeholder availability and approval turnaround. Phases may overlap. We confirm the project plan during scoping.

What can manual data discovery miss?+

Workshops, questionnaires and sampling do not provide an exhaustive automated scan. Undisclosed systems or data stores may remain outside discovery. We document the coverage and assumptions.

What does your bank receive at handover?+

Your bank receives editable registers, approved procedures, training records, open actions and an agreed review schedule. Your teams remain responsible for ongoing operation.
