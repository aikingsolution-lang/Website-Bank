DPDP compliance services for banks

DPDP compliance services your bank can use.

Build your bank’s data protection programme with 11 connected services, from assessment to readiness reporting.

11 connected servicesCompare packages ↓
01

Understand your bank’s DPDP obligations.

Know which DPDP requirements apply to your bank. We review personal data categories, systems and stakeholders, including Significant Data Fiduciary considerations without assuming designation.

Deliverable: DPDP readiness & applicability report
02

Prioritize the gaps that matter.

See where current practices fall short of the agreed DPDP scope. Prioritize action by exposure, customer impact and the effort needed to close each gap.

Deliverable: Gap report, risk register & remediation roadmap
03

Map where personal data goes.

Trace how personal data is collected, stored, used, shared and transferred across banking operations. Departmental workshops and questionnaires build your data inventory, flow diagrams and record of processing activities (RoPA).

Deliverable: Data inventory, flow diagrams & RoPA
04

Give teams clear policies and procedures.

Give each team bank-specific procedures for data protection, retention, rights requests, grievances, vendors and incidents. Name the people responsible for review and approval.

Deliverable: Editable policy & procedure set
05

Make notices and consent clear.

Help customers understand how their data is used. Prepare plain-language notices and consent records for account opening, lending, digital channels and marketing, with workflows for withdrawal, legacy data and minors’ accounts.

Deliverable: Privacy notices, consent wording & workflow templates
06

Review your bank’s security safeguards.

Identify improvements to access management, authentication, encryption, masking, logs, backups and related practices. Your IT teams or chosen vendors use the review findings to plan technical remediation.

Deliverable: Safeguards review & prioritized actions
07

Prepare for personal data breaches.

Know who acts when a personal data breach occurs. Define escalation, response roles, communications and notification templates, then test the plan in a tabletop exercise and record improvements.

Deliverable: Breach response plan, breach log & exercise record
08

Handle rights requests and grievances.

Give teams a consistent process for access, correction, erasure, nomination and grievance requests. Define intake, identity checks, routing and response records.

Deliverable: Rights register, grievance tracker & response templates
09

Manage vendor and processor risks.

Understand which vendors access personal data and the risks involved. Review questionnaires, contractual protections and onboarding controls, supported by a risk-based vendor register.

Deliverable: Vendor register, questionnaires & contract clauses
10

Train teams for their responsibilities.

Help people understand their role in protecting personal data. Brief leadership and train branch, operations, IT, compliance, HR and customer-service teams.

Deliverable: Training materials, attendance & knowledge checks
11

Give leadership a clear readiness view.

Show leadership what is in place and what still needs work. Sample operating evidence, conduct a mock review and track open findings in a final readiness report.

Deliverable: Evidence tracker, readiness report & board summary

Before you decide

Clear answers about the engagement.

Does the engagement include software?+

No. The engagement covers expert-led advisory services. Software procurement, deployment and technical configuration are outside the scope.

Who makes the technical changes?+

Your bank’s IT teams or appointed vendors implement technical remediation. We provide the review findings and guidance.

Can we get ongoing support?+

Optional support includes DPO-as-a-Service, quarterly evidence reviews, refresher training, vendor review support and incident advisory.

Does the engagement guarantee DPDP compliance?+

No. We assess readiness and provide advisory support. Results depend on your bank’s implementation and ongoing operation. The engagement is not a formal legal opinion or independent certification.
