A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Clear from the outset.

Is software included?+

No. This is an expert-led advisory engagement. Software procurement, deployment and technical configuration are outside the scope.

Who implements technical changes?+

Your bank’s IT teams or appointed vendors implement technical remediation. AIKing provides review findings and guidance.

Is ongoing support available?+

Optional support includes DPO-as-a-Service, quarterly evidence reviews, refresher training, vendor review support and incident advisory.

Does the engagement guarantee compliance?+

The engagement provides a readiness assessment and advisory support. Outcomes depend on implementation and ongoing operation by the bank; it is not a formal legal opinion or independent certification.
