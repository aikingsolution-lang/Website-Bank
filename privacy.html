1. Introduction

Aiking Solutions (“Aiking Solutions”, “Aiking”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to protecting personal data and maintaining the confidentiality, integrity and security of information entrusted to us.

We provide technology solutions, cybersecurity services, Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) compliance consulting, privacy assessments, documentation support, risk assessments and related professional services to banks, cooperative banks, cooperative credit societies, financial institutions and other organisations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, receive, access, use, store, disclose, retain and protect personal data in connection with our website, business operations, client engagements and professional services.

This Policy is intended to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (“DPDP Rules”), and other applicable laws, as and when the relevant provisions become applicable.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website and individuals contacting us for enquiries or business opportunities.
  • Representatives, employees, authorised users and designated contacts of our clients.
  • Individuals whose personal data is contained in documents, records or systems made available to us by clients.
  • Vendors, consultants, business partners and service providers.
  • Individuals participating in our assessments, consultations, interviews, training sessions or compliance-related activities.

Where we process personal data strictly on behalf of a client under its instructions, the relevant engagement agreement, data processing terms and the client's own privacy notices may also apply.

3. Categories of Personal Data We May Process

Depending on the nature and scope of an engagement, we may collect or receive the following categories of personal data.

3.1 Business and contact information

  • Name, designation, department and organisation.
  • Business email address, telephone number and business address.
  • Professional correspondence, meeting records and contact preferences.

3.2 Client and compliance documentation

  • Personal data appearing in data inventories, processing activity records, privacy notices, consent records and compliance registers.
  • Information contained in policies, procedures, audit evidence, risk assessments and regulatory documentation.
  • Relevant records concerning data principals, customers, employees, vendors or authorised representatives of a client.

3.3 Technical and security information

  • System and application logs, access records and audit trails.
  • Screenshots, configuration details, vulnerability findings and incident-related records.
  • Device, browser, network and diagnostic information, where applicable.

3.4 Project and engagement information

  • Project correspondence, meeting notes, action trackers and assessment findings.
  • Documents shared through authorised project workspaces or other agreed channels.
  • Information reasonably necessary to deliver the contracted services.

3.5 Website and enquiry information

  • Information submitted through contact forms, email or other communication channels.
  • Website usage information and cookie-related data, where applicable.

We seek to limit the personal data collected or accessed to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not require clients to provide actual customer-level personal data where anonymised, masked or synthetic information is sufficient.

4. Sources of Personal Data

We may obtain personal data:

  • Directly from individuals who contact or engage with us.
  • From client organisations and their authorised representatives.
  • Through authorised access to client systems, applications, documents and project environments.
  • From employees, consultants, vendors or other authorised project participants.
  • Automatically through our website or business technology systems, where applicable.

Clients are expected to share personal data with us only where they have the necessary authority and have satisfied applicable legal, notice, consent or other lawful-processing requirements.

5. Purposes of Processing

We may process personal data for the following specific purposes, as relevant to the engagement:

  • DPDPA compliance consulting: Conducting gap assessments, data mapping, personal data inventory reviews, compliance documentation and implementation planning.
  • Privacy governance: Preparing or reviewing privacy policies, notices, consent frameworks, retention schedules, grievance procedures and data principal rights workflows.
  • Cybersecurity and risk assessments: Reviewing relevant security controls, access management, audit evidence, vulnerabilities and incident response arrangements.
  • Client engagement management: Managing proposals, contracts, project communication, deliverables, meetings and service requests.
  • Legal and regulatory obligations: Maintaining records and responding to lawful requirements applicable to our business or engagement.
  • Security and fraud prevention: Protecting our systems, client information, project environments and business operations against unauthorised access, misuse or security incidents.
  • Website and business administration: Responding to enquiries, managing vendor relationships and improving our services where permitted by applicable law.

We will not use client personal data for unrelated marketing, independent commercial exploitation or other purposes outside the agreed engagement unless separately authorised and legally permitted.

6. Our Role: Data Fiduciary and Service Provider

Our role depends on the activity being performed.

6.1 For our own business activities

Where Aiking Solutions determines the purpose and means of processing personal data—for example, handling website enquiries, managing its business contacts or administering its own operations—we will act as a Data Fiduciary to the extent applicable under the DPDP Act.

6.2 For client engagements

Where we process personal data on behalf of a bank or another client under documented instructions, we will ordinarily act as the client's service provider or data processor, subject to the applicable contractual and legal framework.

In such circumstances, the client will ordinarily determine the purposes and means of processing and remain responsible for its obligations as the relevant Data Fiduciary, where applicable.

Aiking Solutions will process the relevant information only within the agreed scope, authorised instructions and applicable legal requirements.

Our role will be assessed separately for each engagement. The contractual designation of a party will not override its actual role under applicable law.

7. Notice, Consent and Other Lawful Grounds

We will identify and document the applicable lawful grounds for processing personal data in accordance with the DPDP Act and other applicable laws.

Where consent is required, we will seek valid consent through an appropriate process. Where applicable, individuals will be informed of the purposes of processing and the relevant means to exercise their rights or withdraw consent.

Where we process personal data on behalf of a client, the client is responsible for establishing the appropriate lawful basis and providing the required notices or obtaining consent, as applicable. We will reasonably assist the client with these responsibilities within the scope of our engagement.

We will not assume that consent is the only permissible basis for processing in every circumstance; any applicable statutory use or other lawful ground must be assessed against the relevant legal provisions.

8. Disclosure and Sharing of Personal Data

We may share personal data only where necessary for an identified purpose, an authorised engagement or a lawful requirement, including with:

  • The relevant client organisation and its authorised representatives.
  • Employees, consultants and personnel who require access to perform their assigned responsibilities.
  • Approved cloud hosting, document management, communication, security and other technology service providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors or other authorised parties where reasonably necessary.
  • Government authorities, courts, regulators or law enforcement agencies where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted.

Where appropriate, we will use contractual confidentiality obligations, access restrictions and security requirements to protect personal data shared with third parties.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

9. Confidentiality and Security Safeguards

Aiking Solutions will implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards proportionate to the nature of the information, the risks involved and the requirements applicable to the engagement.

Depending on the service and environment, these measures may include:

  • Role-based access control and least-privilege access.
  • Confidentiality commitments for employees, consultants and authorised personnel.
  • Secure transfer and storage mechanisms where appropriate.
  • Encryption and other protections where suitable and supported by the environment.
  • Logging, monitoring and access reviews, where applicable.
  • Secure handling of audit evidence, reports, screenshots and project documentation.
  • Backup, recovery, incident response and secure disposal procedures, as appropriate.
  • Vendor assessment and contractual safeguards for relevant third-party services.

Access to client systems and records will be limited to authorised personnel and the agreed scope of work.

No system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we will take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse of personal data.

10. Personal Data Breach Management

If we become aware of a suspected or confirmed personal data breach involving information processed for a client, we will follow the applicable incident response procedures and contractual reporting requirements.

Where appropriate, we will:

  • Record and assess the incident.
  • Take reasonable steps to contain and investigate it.
  • Notify the affected client within the timeframe specified in the engagement agreement or applicable requirements.
  • Preserve relevant evidence and cooperate with the client's investigation.
  • Support the client with required notifications, remediation and corrective actions within the agreed scope.

For personal data processed in our own capacity, we will assess and fulfil the reporting and notification obligations applicable to Aiking Solutions.

The responsible Data Fiduciary remains accountable for its statutory notification obligations, subject to the applicable law and any duties independently imposed on Aiking Solutions.

11. Retention and Secure Disposal

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or received, subject to applicable legal requirements, contractual obligations and legitimate operational needs.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The duration of the client engagement.
  • The type and sensitivity of the information.
  • Applicable regulatory or statutory recordkeeping requirements.
  • Dispute resolution, legal claims and security investigation needs.
  • Client instructions and agreed project exit procedures.

At the end of an engagement, client information will be returned, deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled in accordance with the engagement agreement and applicable requirements.

Where information must be retained by law, we will restrict its use to the applicable purpose and retain it for the required period.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove every backup copy. Backup data will be handled in accordance with applicable retention and secure-disposal procedures.

12. Data Principal Rights and Requests

Subject to the applicability of the DPDP Act, the nature of the processing and any lawful limitations, individuals may be entitled to:

  • Obtain information about the processing of their personal data.
  • Request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure of personal data where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Seek grievance redressal.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided by applicable law.

Requests concerning personal data held by a bank or another client should ordinarily be directed to that organisation through its designated privacy or grievance channel.

Where Aiking Solutions processes the information on the client's behalf, we will reasonably assist the client in responding to authorised requests in accordance with our contractual obligations.

For personal data processed directly by Aiking Solutions, individuals may contact us using the details in Section 16.

We may request reasonable information to verify a requester's identity and authority before disclosing, correcting or deleting personal data.

13. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential website functionality, security, analytics or other disclosed purposes, depending on the technologies actually deployed.

Where required, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain any necessary consent. Individuals may also manage cookies through their browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

The website owner should update this section to accurately identify the cookies, analytics services and other tracking technologies actually in use.

14. International Transfers and Third-Party Services

Personal data may be processed or stored using third-party technology providers or infrastructure located in India or other jurisdictions, depending on the services selected for a particular engagement.

Any transfer of personal data outside India will be subject to applicable Indian law, government restrictions, contractual commitments and relevant client requirements.

For client engagements, we will not independently select an overseas processing arrangement for client personal data where doing so would exceed our authority or violate the applicable contract.

Clients should be informed of relevant third-party processing arrangements where required.

15. Children's Personal Data

Aiking Solutions does not intentionally seek children's personal data for its ordinary business operations unless it is necessary for an authorised engagement and permitted under applicable law.

Where an engagement involves children's personal data, the parties will assess the applicable requirements relating to parental or lawful guardian consent, age verification, exemptions and other safeguards under the DPDP Act and Rules.

We will follow the relevant client instructions and applicable legal requirements for any such engagement.

17. Client Responsibilities

Organisations engaging Aiking Solutions remain responsible for providing accurate information, defining the scope of the engagement and establishing their own compliance requirements.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, clients are responsible for:

  • Determining the purposes and lawful grounds for their processing activities.
  • Providing appropriate privacy notices and obtaining required consent.
  • Ensuring that personal data shared with us is authorised for disclosure.
  • Maintaining appropriate records, approvals and regulatory registrations, where applicable.
  • Responding to data principal requests and regulatory enquiries within their statutory responsibilities.
  • Informing us of restrictions, retention requirements and relevant security policies.
  • Ensuring that any production data provided for testing or assessment is necessary and appropriately protected.

Our consulting, documentation and assessment services support a client's compliance programme but do not automatically guarantee compliance, certification or regulatory approval.
